# sketchetc · what everything does

## Always on

- **󰀵 apple** · system menu: About, Settings, Lock, Sleep, Reload, notification
  sound, Fullscreen Guard toggle, and *Revert to macOS bar*.
- **󰏘 theme dot** · five theme color dots (click to hot-swap the whole bar) and
  three icon sets: nerd, minimal, emoji.
- **󰨝 widgets** · turn any widget below on or off. The bar allows 9 active at
  a time so it never overflows.
- **front app** · the focused app. Click for a switcher listing running apps.
- **RAM · CPU pill** · live usage. Click either for its top-5 processes and the
  *Clear RAM* button (safely reaps orphaned helpers, then purges and speaks
  the result).
- **battery** · click for time remaining and cycle count.
- **wifi** · click for IP, a Wi-Fi toggle, and Network Settings.
- **volume** · click for a live slider or type an exact 0-100. Scroll the icon
  to nudge.
- **clock** · click opens Calendar.

## Toggleable widgets (defaults marked ●)

- ● **spaces** · desktop spaces 1-4, click to switch.
- ● **clipboard** · copy history (last 5, text and images). Press **Option+V**
  anywhere for the picker: arrow keys + Enter, image previews included.
- ● **pomodoro** · click to start a 25:00 focus timer. Finishing one earns
  aura points scored on your real activity.
- ● **aura** · your effort score. Click for today/week/month totals and
  shareable PNG cards (Export).
- ● **network** · live down/up speed. Click for the top bandwidth users.
- ● **media** · now playing. Click to play/pause, right-click for controls and
  a progress bar.
- ○ **caffeine** · keep the Mac awake, click to toggle.
- ○ **ports** · running dev servers (any port 1024-9999). Click a row to kill.
- ○ **github** · PRs awaiting your review · your open PRs. Click to open.
- ○ **weather** · temperature + air quality, color-coded.
- ○ **speedtest** · one-click internet speed test (Apple's networkQuality).
- ○ **meeting** · "Standup in 8m" appears under an hour out; click joins the
  Zoom/Meet link.
- ○ **focus** · toggle Do Not Disturb (needs a Shortcut named "Toggle Focus").
- ○ **temps** · CPU temperature, plus fan RPM on Macs that have a fan.
- ○ **journal** · tamper-proof daily work log: write in a centered editor,
  entries lock at your cutoff, export any range as markdown.

## Good to know

- Popups behave like native menus: they stay until you click outside, click
  another item, or switch apps.
- Notification sound is customizable from the 󰀵 menu; if the file disappears
  the system default is used.
- Leaving: 󰀵 → Revert to macOS bar (instant, reversible) or `./uninstall.sh`.
